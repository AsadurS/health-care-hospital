Dermatology

Common Skin Problems in Dhaka's Climate and When to See a Dermatologist

Heat, humidity and dust make some skin conditions much more common in Dhaka, especially during the monsoon and summer months.

  • Fungal infections — ringworm and itchy patches in skin folds thrive in damp heat.
  • Prickly heat — tiny red bumps from blocked sweat glands.
  • Acne flare-ups — sweat and pollution clog pores.
  • Eczema and allergies — dust and irritants can trigger dry, itchy skin.

Keep skin dry, wear loose cotton clothes and avoid sharing towels. See a dermatologist if a rash spreads, lasts more than two weeks, oozes, or keeps returning after over-the-counter creams.

Pediatrics

5 Signs Your Child Needs to See a Pediatrician Right Away

  1. High or long fever — any fever in a baby under 3 months, or fever above 102°F lasting more than 2 days.
  2. Fast or difficult breathing — chest pulling in, grunting or blue lips.
  3. Signs of dehydration — very little urine, dry mouth, no tears, sunken eyes.
  4. Unusual drowsiness — hard to wake, floppy or not responding normally.
  5. Rash that does not fade — especially with fever, or spots that stay when pressed.

For breathing difficulty or a child who will not wake, come to Emergency or call 01234567890.

Eye

5 Common Eye Problems in Bangladesh and When to See an Eye Specialist

  • Conjunctivitis (red eye) — spreads quickly in crowded places; wash hands and avoid touching eyes.
  • Refractive errors — blurry vision often needs only glasses; children should be checked before school.
  • Cataract — cloudy vision in older adults, treatable with surgery.
  • Diabetic eye disease — people with diabetes need a yearly eye exam.
  • Dry eye — linked to screens, dust and air-conditioning.

See an eye specialist promptly for sudden vision loss, eye pain, flashes of light, or an eye injury.
