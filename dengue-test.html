সব জ্বরই কি ডেঙ্গু জ্বর?

Not every fever is dengue. Dengue fever often runs at 101–102°F, may be continuous, or may drop with sweating and return. Body aches, headache, pain behind the eyes and a reddish rash can appear — though dengue is possible even without these signs.
